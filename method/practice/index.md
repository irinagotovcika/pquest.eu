    <h2>Practice before it matters.</h2>

    <p>
      Knowledge is essential, but knowing what to do is not always the same as
      being able to use that knowledge when a situation unfolds.
    </p>

    <p>
      In fields such as healthcare and aviation, simulation- and scenario-based
      training create opportunities to practice skills and decisions in realistic
      contexts before they are needed in real-world situations.<sup><a href="{{ '/method/evidence/#ref-1' | relative_url }}">[1]</a>,<a href="{{ '/method/evidence/#ref-2' | relative_url }}">[2]</a></sup>
    </p>

    <p>
      PQuest builds on the broader principle behind this approach: practice
      should not begin only when the real situation arrives.
    </p>

    <p>
      The PQuest Method brings active, contextual practice to psychological
      skills — creating opportunities to engage with situations, make decisions,
      encounter change, and adapt within the training environment.
    </p>
